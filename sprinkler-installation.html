Sprinkler System Installation in the Treasure Valley

Idaho gets about 12 inches of rain a year. Without a sprinkler system your lawn is not making it through July. We've been installing systems across Nampa, Boise, Meridian and the rest of the valley since 2015.

Every install gets the same process. We walk the yard, check your water pressure, lay out the zones, put it in, pressure test it, and show you how it all works before we leave.

New Construction

The best time to put sprinklers in is before the sod goes down. The grade is set, trenching is clean, and it costs less than coming back later. We work with builders and GCs to fit the install into the build schedule.

Building in Nampa, Caldwell, Meridian or Star? Call us early and we'll get on the schedule before landscaping starts.

Existing Yards

Adding sprinklers to a yard that's already in takes more care. We cut the lines in, keep the mess down, and put the sod back when we're done. Most existing yards take 1 to 2 days.

New Construction Install

Most of the valley is heavy clay. It soaks up water slow, so if a zone runs too long the water ends up in the street. We set head spacing and run times for our soil, not some national default, so you don't get puddles in one spot and dead grass in the corners.

Bad zone layout, wrong pipe size and heads spaced too far apart are what cause most coverage problems. We sort that out on paper before we dig.

Before You Hire Anyone, Ask These Questions

A lot of the problems we fix on newer systems come from installs where somebody cut corners to hit a low price. Ask every company these before you sign anything:

  1. Where do you buy your parts? You want an irrigation supply house, not the big box store. Box store heads and valves are usually the homeowner line, not the contractor grade stuff.
  2. What brand are you installing? Get the brand and model written on the quote. Stick with name brands like Rain Bird or Hunter so parts are easy to find when something needs fixed down the road.
  3. Is there a shutoff (curb stop) where the system ties in? Some installers just tee into your water line with no shutoff. Then any time the sprinklers need work, water to the whole house has to go off.
  4. Do you pressure test before backfill? If they bury it without testing, you find the leaks later as soggy spots in the lawn.
  5. Is the backflow preventer in the price? Most systems need one. A low quote that leaves it out is not a real quote.
  6. Are you licensed and insured? Ask for proof. If someone gets hurt or a line gets hit, you don't want that on you.

We buy from irrigation supply houses, install Rain Bird and other name brand parts, put a shutoff on the sprinkler tie-in, and pressure test every system. Ask us any of these. We're happy to answer.

Common Questions

Sprinkler Installation FAQ

How much does a new sprinkler system cost in the Treasure Valley? +
Most installs run $1,400 to $1,850 per zone. A typical neighborhood home in Nampa, Meridian, Boise or Caldwell needs 3 to 4 zones, so most jobs land between $4,200 and $7,400. Lot size, water pressure and drip for beds move the number. We give you a firm price after a free on-site estimate.
How many irrigation zones do I need? +
It depends on lot size, water pressure and your landscaping. Most neighborhood homes are 3 to 4 zones. Bigger lots with more lawn, beds and drip need more. We figure it out at the free estimate by measuring the yard and checking your pressure.
How long does sprinkler installation take? +
Most homes take 1 to 2 days. Bigger or more complicated yards can take 3. New construction before sod usually goes faster than an existing yard.
Can you install sprinklers in an existing yard? +
Yes. We trench the lines in, keep the mess down, and put the sod back when we're done.
Do I need a backflow preventer? +
Most systems need one to keep sprinkler water out of the drinking water. We figure out the right device at the estimate and it's included in every install.
When is the best time to install a sprinkler system? +
For new construction, before the sod goes down. For existing yards, spring through fall works as long as the ground isn't frozen. Spring books up fast, so call early.
Do you pull the permit? +
Yes, when one is required. We're a licensed and insured Idaho contractor and handle permits for Boise, Meridian, Nampa and the surrounding cities.
What should I ask before hiring a sprinkler installation company? +
Ask where they buy their parts, what brand they install, whether they put a shutoff on the sprinkler tie-in, whether they pressure test before backfill, whether the backflow preventer is in the price, and if they're licensed and insured.
